# Adrian

Adrian is a Farisi contact the party meets while seeking information about airships, gems, and travel.

He is immediately interested in the materials and possibilities the party presents, especially once [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] and [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]] draw him into conversation.

Adrian points the party toward Captain Martin and the Dawn's Pearl as a possible airship contact.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Helps bridge the party from seaship contacts into airship contacts.
- Shows how tightly Farisi gem culture and airship travel are connected.
