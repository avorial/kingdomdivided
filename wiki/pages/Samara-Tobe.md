# Samara Tobe

Samara Tobe is a House Tobe figure encountered during the party's flight through Lumia.

She appears in bronze armor with green emeralds, identifies herself as a member of the House of Wind, and offers House Tobe support while warning that Obsidian-aligned forces are occupying or threatening parts of Tobe territory.

## Known Information

- Associated with House Tobe's military arm.
- Knows enough about the Lumia crisis to move with urgency.
- Can move the party from house to house or toward ships.
- Advises that sailing south may be safer than trying to cross compromised territory.
