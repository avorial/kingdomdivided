# Tiberius

Tiberius is first mate of the [[Kempenfelt]] and the main face of the Heller giant crew while [[Ronan]] is injured.

He is grateful for the party's effort to obtain medicine for Ronan and negotiates passage for them toward [[Hodong]] and [[Farisi]].

## Role In The Chronicle

- Handles negotiation while Ronan is incapacitated.
- Helps explain the Heller crew's situation and [[Valens]]' frenzy.
- Becomes a key contact for the party among the giants.
