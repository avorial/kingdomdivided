# Mikado Session 04 - Flight to Hanan

The party tries to leave Lumia as Obsidian danger spreads. They encounter House Tobe troops and [[Samara Tobe]], who offers movement, protection, and hard truths about Tobe territory.

The group increasingly understands that the Obsidian problem is not only local. Shadows and reflections can be stolen or altered, and the implications may reach across continents.

## Important Beats

- House Tobe forces intercept or assist the party.
- Samara Tobe identifies herself as House of Wind.
- The party considers whether to move house-to-house or sail south.
- Thorn's missing or altered shadow becomes part of the mystery.
- The group recognizes that Obsidian influence may create shadow reflections or remove reflections entirely.
- Passage toward House Hanan territory becomes the safest path forward.
