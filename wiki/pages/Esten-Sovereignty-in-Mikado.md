# Esten Sovereignty in Mikado

The Mikado Chronicle repeatedly returns to the question of Esten sovereignty inside the [[Mikado Empire]].

Esten communities have lived in regions such as [[Lumia]] for centuries. They retain ritual authority, local leadership, sea songs, ancestor practices, and a sense of peoplehood that does not fully collapse into Mikado imperial identity.

## Key Tensions

- Mikado authority claims taxes, military law, and imperial legitimacy.
- Esten leaders claim cultural continuity and local trust.
- Some Esten see the return of old houses, including Obsidian, as possible liberation.
- Others fear that old house power will repeat the disasters of the Belllands.
- Returning Esten bloodlines from the Belllands complicate local claims.

## Why It Matters

The party cannot solve Lumia by choosing only law or only culture. Every route through the region requires deciding whose authority matters and what kind of future the Esten are allowed to have.
