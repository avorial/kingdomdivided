# Mikado Session 02 - Lumia and Chabat

The party lands on the southern coast of the Mikado Empire in [[Lumia]], one of the Esten domains. Their ship leaves quickly before inspectors can become a problem.

They enter [[Chabat]], a city built in or around a previously erupted volcano. The group deals with lodging, local authority, the prefect's people, Esten community leaders, and the choice of whether to build power through Mikado channels or Esten channels.

## Important Beats

- The party arrives on a pirate or irregular ship.
- They encounter local inspectors and questions about travel legitimacy.
- Esten inns, sea shanties, and community leaders give the region texture.
- [[Edral Quedil]] and [[Valerian]] become possible Esten contacts.
- The party learns about the local prefect, taxes, and stolen or mishandled goods.
- Leads emerge about Leandan workers, House Hanan papers, and a large dig.
