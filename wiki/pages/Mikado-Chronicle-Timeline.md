# Mikado Chronicle Timeline

## Game Zero

- Character concepts are built around Mikado houses, Esten identity, Worm God faith, House Maj prophecy, House Tobe reform, shadow assassination, and exile.
- The core party begins taking shape around [[Mei Ling]], [[Nikos]], [[Thorn]], [[Diogenes]], [[Rina]], and allied retainers.
- The political wound of the Belllands, the Sedai, and the Mikado return is established.

## Game 0.5

- The characters gather under letters and seals connected to the Sedai/Casamante fallout.
- [[Rina]] sends [[Diogenes]] to find [[Thorn]].
- The group meets in Oldwin and prepares for audience with the Sedai Throne.

## Session 1 - Exile And Sea Passage

- Mikado characters and Bellringer companions are exiled from the Belllands.
- House staff are forced onto ships in a politically ugly evacuation.
- The party travels by river and sea, encountering Covingard protection, pirates, Esten ship-superstitions, and a Mikado warship.
- The group chooses to approach Mikado lands through [[Lumia]].

## Session 2 - Lumia And Chabat

- The party lands near the southern coast of the Mikado, in Esten-majority [[Lumia]].
- They arrive in [[Chabat]], a city built in or around a previously erupted volcano.
- They deal with the prefect's people, Esten leaders, inns, sea shanties, local taxation, and the choice between Mikado authority and Esten authority.
- The first leads about Leandan digging, illegal movement, and Obsidian influence appear.

## Session 3 - Esten Rituals And The Dig

- The party learns about the House of Obsidian and Shadow, old Esten rule, and why some Esten may see Obsidian return as liberation.
- A large dig site tied to Leandan workers, House Hanan papers, and forbidden travel becomes a major concern.
- Obsidian corruption is linked to shadows, reflections, and possession-like unity.

## Session 4 - Flight From Lumia

- The party flees through Lumia while Obsidian-controlled forces and House Tobe troops move.
- [[Samara Tobe]] appears and offers House Tobe support.
- The party reaches ships that can move them toward House Hanan or deeper Mikado territory.
- Shadow loss, stolen reflections, and the possibility of Obsidian influence across continents become explicit.

## Final Mikado Session

- The party lands in [[House Hanan]] territory under protection.
- House Tobe arrives and challenges Hanan secrecy.
- The head of House Tobe reveals or implies that the Emperor may have fallen or that the Imperial House is compromised.
- Obsidian glass, masks, and corruption are tied back to the Belllands' old Obsidian War.
- The party resolves the immediate question of the Obsidian taint and leaves the arc with the Mikado Empire in crisis.
