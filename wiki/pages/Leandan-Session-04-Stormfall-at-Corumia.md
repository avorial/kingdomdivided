# Leandan Session 04 - Stormfall at Corumia

## Synopsis

The Victory's route goes wrong when a hurricane blows the ship across the sound and off course. The vessel is damaged but not destroyed: water sloshes aboard and repairs are needed, but it is not sinking.

The party finds itself in the Protectorate of Corumia, between Samin and Derovnia, near the storm-struck village of [[Davin]]. The crew has to get close enough to shore to make repairs without crashing into another damaged ship already in the area.

That second ship is the [[Kempenfelt]], crewed by Heller giants. The local village is not openly hostile to the giants, but the welcome is cold. The party begins to see that prejudice, fear, and practical need are all tangled together here.

[[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian]] offers his repair skills to the storm-damaged village, while the others begin looking for lodging, information, and ways to get back toward [[Farisi]] and the original mission.

## Important Threads

- The party is blown far off its planned route.
- The Protectorate of Corumia becomes an unexpected crossroads.
- Heller giants and the crew of the Kempenfelt enter the story.
- Sebastian's usefulness as a repairer and inventor earns local goodwill.
