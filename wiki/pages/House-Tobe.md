# House Tobe

House Tobe is a former Ai Hoku house and one of the central military/political forces in the Mikado Chronicle.

The house carries old greatness, old guilt, and a reformist future. It is connected to archers, military authority, and the question of whether the old Shogunate order can return in a new form.

## Known Events

- [[Samara Tobe]] meets the party while fleeing or moving through Lumia.
- House Tobe troops appear during the escape from Obsidian-influenced territory.
- The head of House Tobe confronts House Hanan to reach the party.
- House Tobe initiates or supports the claim that the Emperor or Imperial House may have fallen.

## Political Position

Tobe's knowledge may be necessary, but its methods risk looking like ambition or treason. The house is willing to move before the Imperial Court can fully manage the story.
