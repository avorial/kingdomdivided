# Leandan Session 05 - The Giant Captain

## Synopsis

The party learns that [[Ronan]], the half-giant captain of the [[Kempenfelt]], was badly injured in the storm and may have a concussion. The local villagers are not eager to help the Heller crew, so [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]] agrees to seek medical aid despite not being a physician.

The party visits an apothecary run by [[Nadia]] and her daughter [[Roya]]. They secure a mild green healing potion, a fire starter kit, and an unusually promising uncut fire gemstone.

[[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] suggests a bargain: medical help for Ronan in exchange for assistance with ship repairs or passage. The giants are grateful enough for the attempt that negotiation becomes possible.

Meanwhile [[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian]] stays with local repair workers and continues earning trust through practical help. The rest of the party returns to trader lodging while waiting for medicine and the next step.

## Important Threads

- Violet becomes the link between the party and the injured giant captain.
- Nadia and Roya's apothecary becomes a useful local resource.
- Mercer reframes compassion into leverage without fully losing the human stakes.
- The fire gemstone becomes an important technical and material find.
