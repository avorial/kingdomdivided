# Mikado Session 06 - House Hanan and the Imperial Crisis

The party reaches [[House Hanan]] territory and is sheltered in a protected Hanan home or castle. The security is immediate and intense.

House Tobe arrives and demands access. Hanan refuses to casually allow it, because the matter touches imperial protection and possibly the legitimacy of the Imperial House itself.

The head of [[House Tobe]] eventually communicates urgent knowledge: the Emperor or Imperial House may have fallen, been compromised, or become impossible to trust. This would imply a crisis that Hanan is institutionally built not to believe.

## Important Beats

- Hanan guards protect the party from Tobe access.
- House Tobe insists the matter is urgent and imperial.
- The party must decide whether to leave Hanan protection with Tobe.
- Obsidian masks, glass, and corruption are tied back to the Belllands' old history.
- The session clarifies that the Obsidian crisis is not local to Chabat.
- The arc ends with the Mikado Empire facing a potentially existential legitimacy crisis.
