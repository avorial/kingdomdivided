# Leandan Session 02 - The Sky Crystal Job

## Synopsis

The second session begins with [[Matthew Rousseau]] collecting signed papers in an upstairs room at [[The Brass Cog]]. He leaves the party with provisions, drinks, and the uneasy knowledge that they have accepted a job whose politics are larger than the first offer made it sound.

Rousseau explains the shape of the problem. A seat on Leandan's Small Council is up for election and is expected to go to the Shipwrights Guild. That expected result could be disrupted if the Airship Guild can be given an opportunity, but the Airship Guild and Shipwrights Guild are tangled together under the same political umbrella.

The key to the pressure is the sky crystal supply. Sky crystals are produced only in [[Cataclistos]], and they make airship travel possible. That monopoly gives the Shipwrights and Airship Guild enormous control over commerce, shipping, and access.

The party spends the session learning one another's edges, clarifying why the Jewelers Guild may want outside help, and beginning to plan how to approach Cataclistos without looking like exactly what they are.

## Important Threads

- The Sky Crystal problem is both economic and political.
- Cataclistos is reclusive and difficult to enter casually.
- The Airship Guild may be a target, tool, or ally depending on what the party discovers.
- The party starts considering cover stories and a less-than-public investigation.
