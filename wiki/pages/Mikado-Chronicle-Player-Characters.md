# Mikado Chronicle Player Characters

This is the working index for the Mikado Chronicle party.

## Named Characters

- [[Mei Ling]] - House Maj noble and speaker shaped by prophecy, Worm God faith, and the burden of speaking for others.
- [[Nikos]] - a changed badger-like militant missionary and Worm God convert with claws, digging, and fierce devotion.
- [[Thorn]] - a shadow assassin whose lack of self drives a need for connection.
- [[Diogenes]] - an Esten scholar, teacher, and cultural bridge.
- [[Rina]] - a noble organizer who draws the group together during the Oldwin opening.
- [[Lorena]] - a Tobe reformist concept tied to House Tobe's old greatness and future direction.

## Notes

Some names and spellings vary in the transcripts. This page should be tightened as the final character sheets are confirmed.
