# The Victory

The Victory is the Shipwrights Guild vessel the Leandan party books for the first leg of the journey toward [[Farisi]] and [[Cataclistos]].

The ship is captained by [[Sarah Van Jansen]], with [[Brom Baker]] serving as first mate. It provides the party's first close look at seaship guild culture, shipboard labor, passenger travel, and the politics of maritime Leandan.

## Chronicle Events

- The party boards the Victory in [[Leandan Session 03 - The Victory]].
- [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] gambles with crew and captain aboard the ship.
- A hurricane blows the Victory off course in [[Leandan Session 04 - Stormfall at Corumia]].
- The damaged ship reaches the shallows near the Protectorate of Corumia.
