# Leandan Session 03 - The Victory

## Synopsis

The party begins in [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]]'s shop, where they refine their information away from public ears. [[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian]] creates a clever jeweler's aid from castoffs at Violet's desk, giving her a better way to examine crystals and their properties.

The group debates how to reach [[Cataclistos]]. They consider the city's reputation for reclusiveness and decide they need a cover story strong enough to pass through the gates. The working approach is to present [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] as an up-and-coming designer or guild-connected opportunity, supported by Sebastian's devices and Violet's gem expertise.

They choose to travel by boat around the Cape of [[Farisi]], landing near a port that can put them on a path toward Cataclistos. They secure passage on [[The Victory]], a Shipwrights Guild vessel captained by [[Sarah Van Jansen]] with [[Brom Baker]] as first mate.

On board, Mercer works the social field, gambling with crew and captain while learning what he can about ship culture, guild expectations, and possible future leverage.

## Important Threads

- The party's cover becomes a business/design approach rather than a pure infiltration plan.
- The Victory provides their first close contact with Shipwrights Guild culture.
- Mercer begins building ties through gambling and charm.
- Sebastian and Violet's technical credibility becomes central to the planned approach.
