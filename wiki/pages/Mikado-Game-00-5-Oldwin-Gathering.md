# Mikado Game 0.5 - Oldwin Gathering

The introduction session places the finished characters in Oldwin before the full arc begins.

Letters, seals, and political messengers draw the characters toward the same meeting. [[Rina]] works through intermediaries, sending [[Diogenes]] to locate [[Thorn]]. The group prepares for conversation with the Sedai Throne and the political consequences of the Belllands crisis.

## Important Beats

- The Seal of the Sedai and Casamante-linked messages frame the opening.
- Rina gathers people with status, danger, and specialized knowledge.
- Thorn is approached as a useful but risky ally.
- Mei Ling and Nikos demonstrate Mikado etiquette and identity.
- The session positions the party for exile and the Mikado return.
