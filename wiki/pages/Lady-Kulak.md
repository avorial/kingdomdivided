# Lady Kulak

Lady Kulak is a Knight of Covingard who appears during the sea passage out of the Belllands.

She identifies herself as sent by Duke Louis to make sure the will of Covingard is carried out. In practical terms, she is there to protect the party from those who would use the exile as an opportunity to harm them.
