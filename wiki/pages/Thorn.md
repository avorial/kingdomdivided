# Thorn

Thorn is a shadow assassin in the [[Mikado Chronicle]]. His concept is built around shadow movement, assassination, and a lack of self that makes connection both difficult and necessary.

During the Obsidian arc, Thorn's relationship to shadow becomes more than a character trick. The party notices that stolen or missing shadows may be part of the larger [[Obsidian Taint]].

## Known Threads

- Can shadow jump or move through shadow.
- Is approached by [[Diogenes]] on behalf of [[Rina]] during the Oldwin opening.
- Becomes a key lens for understanding stolen shadows and reflection loss.

## Connections

- [[Rina]]
- [[Diogenes]]
- [[Obsidian Taint]]

### Aspects

- **Emperor's Shadow-Jumping Blade Who Steals Identities:** assassinates enemies of the Empire to preserve stability in the Bellands, taking identities from those he kills to climb Mikado influence.
- **Haunted by the Kayna Echoes of Other's Past:** stress can pull him toward the fears or goals of identities he has worn.
- **Saltblood Veins, Shadow-Jump Rites:** Kayna blood and Bellringer magic power his shadow jumping, but the result feels alien far from home.
- **Covering Tracks with a Dead Man's Face:** useful when a stolen identity is suspected or when Rina's escape is at stake.
- **A Thousand Faces, No True Self:** perfect infiltration hides a personal void and a hunger for fleeting connection.
- **A Tiger in the Night:** touched by the Worm Gods and connected to darkness.

### Stunts

- **Shadow Flicker:** spend a Fate Point to briefly teleport from one human-sized shadow to another, even through solid objects or over short distances. Usually gives +2 to Stealth for impossible movement, or +2 to Fight when using the flicker to create combat advantage.
- **Perfect Impersonation:** after taking a target's identity, usually by killing them and taking flesh, gain a temporary aspect reflecting that person's dominant quality until Thorn sheds it.
- **Echoes of the Deep:** spend a Fate Point to draw on Saltblood and Kayna heritage for insight into fear, water, the sea, or ancient things; may use Lore instead of Empathy for that kind of psychological advantage.
