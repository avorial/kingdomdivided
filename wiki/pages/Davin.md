# Davin

Davin is the village near the shallows where [[The Victory]] lands after being blown off course by a hurricane.

The village is in the Protectorate of Corumia, between Samin and Derovnia. It has storm damage, limited lodging, and a wary relationship with the Heller giants whose ship, the [[Kempenfelt]], is also damaged nearby.

## Chronicle Use

- Landing point after the hurricane.
- Home of [[Xander Wong]].
- Site of local repairs, lodging, and the apothecary run by [[Nadia]] and [[Roya]].
