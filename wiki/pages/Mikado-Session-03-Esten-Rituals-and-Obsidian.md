# Mikado Session 03 - Esten Rituals and Obsidian

The party digs deeper into the Esten side of Lumia. They learn that the House of Obsidian and Shadow once ruled or dominated Esten history, and that some Esten see its return as a path toward independence from Mikado control.

At the same time, the party investigates the Leandan dig and the strange permissions attached to it.

## Important Beats

- The party learns about the House of Obsidian and Shadow.
- Some Esten believe Obsidian return could restore power or sovereignty.
- House Tobe is discussed as a house that experiments with dangerous change.
- The group learns that the dig may be tied to Hanan papers and illegal travel.
- The first strong signs appear that Obsidian influence acts through reflection, shadow, and unity of behavior.
