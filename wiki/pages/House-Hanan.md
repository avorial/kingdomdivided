# House Hanan

House Hanan is the Mikado guardian house tied to imperial protection, courtier service, and direct defense of the Imperial order.

In the Mikado Chronicle, House Hanan escorts and shelters the party. Their protection is powerful but also restrictive: they control access, manage information, and resist House Tobe's attempt to force a conversation.

## Known Role

- Provides courtiers, bodyguards, and protected houses.
- Holds a guarded border before deeper Mikado lands.
- Protects the party after their escape through Lumia.
- Is deeply reluctant to believe claims that the Emperor or Imperial House has fallen.

## Conflict With House Tobe

House Tobe arrives demanding access to the party and claims urgent knowledge. Hanan refuses to casually let another house override its protection, especially when the matter touches the Imperial House.

## Open Questions

- How much does Hanan know about the [[Obsidian Taint]]?
- Are they protecting the party, controlling the party, or both?
- Who inside Hanan can be trusted if the Imperial House is compromised?
