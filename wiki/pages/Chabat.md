# Chabat

Chabat is a city in [[Lumia]], built in or around a previously erupted volcano. The party reaches it early in the Mikado Chronicle.

Chabat becomes the first major place where the party sees the fracture between Esten communities, Mikado authority, local prefectural power, and the emerging [[Obsidian Taint]].

## Known Events

- The party arrives after being dropped off by a pirate or irregular ship.
- Local inspectors, prefectural concerns, and Esten leaders all become relevant.
- The group hears Esten sea shanties and begins building local relationships.
- The city contains or connects to an obsidian coliseum or volcanic structure.
- The party later discovers hidden seams, obsidian steps, prison spaces, and Obsidian-altered people.

## Open Questions

- Who first allowed Obsidian influence into Chabat?
- How deeply is the local prefecture compromised?
- What was being dug up, and who paid for it?
