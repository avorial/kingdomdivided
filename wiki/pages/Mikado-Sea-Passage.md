# Mikado Sea Passage

The sea passage carries the exiled Mikado party from the Belllands toward the [[Mikado Empire]].

The journey includes river movement, pirate danger, Mikado warships, Esten ship superstition, and tense decisions about where to land.

## Known Events

- The party boards ships while other Mikado staff are also pushed aboard.
- A Covingard knight, Lady Kulak, identifies herself as a protector sent by Duke Louis.
- Nikos throws a naked attacker overboard during a shipboard confrontation.
- Esten presence aboard ship is treated as auspicious because of the legend of the first Esten and her stone lover.
- A Mikado warship intercepts or approaches.
- The group chooses a route toward [[Lumia]] rather than sailing directly into the most watched imperial waters.

## Related Song

The transcript includes an Esten sea shanty beginning with white sails rising and oars beating hollow. It belongs with this journey and with the Esten sailors' sense of loss, return, and uncertain stars.
