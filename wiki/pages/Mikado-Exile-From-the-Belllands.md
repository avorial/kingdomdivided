# Mikado Exile From the Belllands

The Mikado Chronicle begins in the aftermath of the Casamante and Covingard crisis. The Mikado characters and their Bellringer companions are told they are being exiled from the Belllands.

The stated reason is political survival: if Mikado landings or military action begin, public violence against every Mikado in the nation could erupt.

## Known Events

- Mikado nobles are awakened by troops outside their doors.
- The party is taken into the Imperial Seat and told they must leave.
- House staff and servants are forced onto ships, creating immediate moral and logistical tension.
- Covingard knights appear to ensure the will of Duke Louis/Covingard is carried out.
- The party is moved by ship toward the Mikado Empire.

## Consequence

The exile turns the party from guests or political actors in the Belllands into unstable returnees in Mikado politics.
