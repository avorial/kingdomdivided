# Mikado Session 05 - Chabat Underworld

The party investigates the hidden structure beneath [[Chabat]]. They find seams, descending steps, obsidian surfaces, and prison-like spaces.

Inside, they encounter altered people: an Esten woman in Mikado clothing, a Mikado priest, a normal Mikado person, an Obsidian-altered woman, and at least one of the [[Maw People]]. The Maw person fights the Obsidian and uses shattered obsidian as a weapon.

## Important Beats

- A hidden seam leads downward.
- Sandstone gives way to obsidian.
- Polished obsidian becomes a direct danger.
- Obsidian shards absorb power.
- The Maw person appears to hate or oppose the Obsidian.
- The structure collapses or destabilizes, creating fear that the volcano has become active.
