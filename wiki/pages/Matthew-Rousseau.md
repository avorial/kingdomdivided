# Matthew Rousseau

Matthew Rousseau is the Jewelers Guild-connected fixer who gathers the Leandan party at [[The Brass Cog]].

He presents the job as a deniable opportunity. The Jewelers Guild needs people willing to look into the sky crystal problem, [[Cataclistos]], the Airship Guild, and the political leverage around an upcoming Small Council seat.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Sends feelers into respectable and shady circles.
- Meets the party in an upstairs room at [[The Brass Cog]].
- Collects signed papers after explaining the broad shape of the mission.
- Frames the work as political, economic, and guild-sensitive.
