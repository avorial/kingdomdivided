# Captain Martin

Captain Martin is connected to the Dawn's Pearl, an airship contact mentioned during the party's time in [[Farisi]].

The Dawn's Pearl is described as an airship that takes passengers and ships high-value cargo, especially gems and luxury goods. Martin and the ship become a possible route deeper into the Airship Guild side of the Leandan Chronicle.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Potential airship contact.
- Possible path toward understanding sky crystal travel.
- Tied to the commercial airship network around Farisi.
