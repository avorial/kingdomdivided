# Xander Wong

Xander Wong is the mayor of [[Davin]], the storm-struck village where the party lands after the hurricane drives [[The Victory]] off course.

He discusses transportation options with the party while the ship is damaged and the group is trying to determine how to get back toward [[Farisi]].

## Role In The Chronicle

- Local authority in Davin.
- Helps establish the practical situation after the hurricane.
- Points toward options such as waiting for repairs or finding another trader.
