#### Personal History

Litlia Mookjai, the first daughter and esteemed heir of House Mookjai, embodies the essence of resilience and saltblood cultural pride. In her formative years, she shared an exceptionally close bond with Ophelia Ulami, a childhood companion who was more like a sister than a servant. Under Ophelia's influence, Litlia delved into the ancient songs and ventured beyond the confines of noble halls, discovering the world's back alleys and the impoverished corners of her homeland. These experiences were instrumental in shaping her perspective, transforming her into a compassionate advocate for her people.
Her father, the Count, welcomed the connections she fostered, hopeful that they would endear her to the saltblood community. Yet, he could not foresee the tragedy that would later transform Litlia into a fervent advocate for her people's cause.
During a visit to Mistwick at the age of 17, Litlia asked Ophelia to procure refreshments. When her return was delayed, Litlia, from the heights of a castle tower, witnessed a horrifying scene: a frenzied mob brutally attacked and tore Ophelia apart. The mob justified their actions by accusing her of theft, fueled by misunderstandings arising from Ophelia's limited knowledge of the Bellringer tongue. The incident exacerbated by racial biases, and the Pearstone people's perception that all Saltbloods as dishonest thieves.
Even the Duchess offered a personal apology, but Litlia was left in a state of shock for weeks. Upon returning home, she remained inconsolable, despite her father's well-intentioned efforts. Desperate to help his daughter recover, the Count allowed a mystic who visited the castle to meet with Litlia. This mystic guided Litlia on a path of communion with the land, teaching her to hear the ancestral song of her people in the whispers of stone and water. These spiritual connections provided solace in a way no person could.
Once her inner wounds began to heal, Litlia embraced her role as a champion of the saltblood people. She devoted herself to bridging the chasm between the tribal poverty of her community and the opulence of high society. With a profound appreciation for the challenges faced by marginalized Almiern tribes, Litlia tirelessly advocated for their cultural heritage and inclusion in the broader social fabric of the Duchy.
Her remarkable talent as a singer allowed her to showcase the rich tapestry of saltblood traditions and rituals. Through her evocative performances, she brought to life the vibrant history and mystical allure of her people, captivating audiences far and wide.
Litlia also delved into the realm of spiritual summoning, deepening her connection with the land. She could channel the ancient spirits of the Almiern territories, both safeguarding her people and seeking their guidance in times of turmoil and uncertainty.
Balancing the interests of her people with the demands of foreign powers posed a daunting challenge. Litlia navigated the intricacies of diplomacy and statecraft, tirelessly advocating for alliances that emphasized mutual respect and preserved the unique identity and cultural integrity of the saltblood community.
Litlia Mookjai's journey, marked by tragedy and resilience, continues to inspire generations, establishing her as a trailblazer in cross-cultural diplomacy and cooperation.
Litlia Mookjai's journey as a cultural luminary, spiritual advocate, and diplomatic envoy continues to shape the narrative of House Mookjai and the saltblood communities, serving as a beacon of hope and inspiration for generations to come.
