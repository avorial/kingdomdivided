# Mikado Session 01 - Exile and Sea

The Mikado characters are awakened by troops and brought into the political machinery of the Belllands. They are told that they, like all Mikado, are being exiled from the Belllands.

The reason is presented as containment: if Mikado forces land, common anger and religious fear may turn into mass violence against every Mikado in the nation.

## Important Beats

- The party is told to leave the Belllands.
- House staff and dependents are pushed onto ships.
- The group sees how ugly exile is for people below noble rank.
- [[Lady Kulak]] appears as a Covingard knight protecting the party.
- Shipboard danger, pirates, and Mikado warships complicate the journey.
- Esten ship-superstitions become important, especially the belief that an Esten aboard is a blessing against sinking.
- The party chooses to approach the [[Mikado Empire]] through [[Lumia]].
