### Foundry Notes

#### Personal History

Young Philip was born to a wealthy weaponsmith of the House Covinggard. They were able to save enough for their son to join the Church of the Bells. Because he did not have any strong noble connections he had to make his own way, the way he found was going on the ships of of people from the Bellringer nation exploring other domains for trade.

This has given Philip a strong understand of the heretic magics of the sea people. Though time and reputation he has now become a troubleshooter of sorts supporting nobles when dark magics come in to play
