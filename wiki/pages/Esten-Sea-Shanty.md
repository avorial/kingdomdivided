# Esten Sea Shanty

The Mikado Chronicle transcripts include a short Esten sea shanty connected to the sea passage and Lumia sessions.

## Fragment

White sails rise, ready where eyes keep. Carry us home through the sorrowful deep. The oars beat hollow, the tide runs black. The stars of our fathers will not guide us back.

## Meaning

The song fits the arc's emotional center: exile, uncertain return, and a people whose ancestral signs no longer guarantee safety.
