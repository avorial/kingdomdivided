# Lumia

Lumia is an Esten-majority region of the [[Mikado Empire]]. The party lands there after the exile and sea passage from the Belllands.

The region is politically complicated. Mikado authority claims tax, law, and military control, but Esten communities have lived there for centuries and retain their own rituals, leadership, and loyalties.

## Known Features

- Southern coastal trade reaches into Lumia.
- [[Chabat]] is one of the major cities encountered by the party.
- The Esten population is strong enough that local politics cannot be reduced to imperial command.
- House Lulumia and other Esten authorities matter on the ground.
- Obsidian influence appears among Esten communities and buried sites.

## Political Problem

The party repeatedly has to choose whether to deal with Mikado authority, Esten authority, or both. Stronger Esten connections make movement through Lumia easier, while Mikado connections carry legal weight and danger.
