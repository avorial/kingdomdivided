# Mikado Chronicle

The Mikado Chronicle follows the Mikado nobles, Esten allies, and Bellringer-connected escorts after the Belllands exile them from the Imperial Seat.

The arc begins with the group being removed from the Belllands after the meeting with Casamante and Covingard. They are placed on ships with staff, guards, and political uncertainty, then carried toward the [[Mikado Empire]].

The campaign quickly becomes more than a homecoming. Lumia is unstable, Esten sovereignty is contested, House Tobe and House Hanan are maneuvering, and the ancient threat of the [[Obsidian Taint]] is rising again.

## Major Threads

- Exile from the Belllands and return to Mikado lands.
- The political position of Esten-descended houses in the Mikado Empire.
- The return or awakening of the House of Obsidian and Shadow.
- House Hanan's protection, secrecy, and proximity to the Imperial House.
- House Tobe's attempt to act before the Imperial Court can control the story.
- The discovery that Obsidian influence can steal or distort reflection, shadow, and identity.
- The question of whether the Maw people are enemies, rivals of the Obsidian, or possible allies.
