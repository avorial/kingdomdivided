# Leandan Session 01 - The Brass Cog

## Synopsis

The first Leandan game introduces the core party and the shady-but-useful tone of the chronicle.

[[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer Templeton]], sometimes called Peck, presents himself as a verbose and connected member of a jewel-crafting family: the sort of man who knows someone who knows someone, and always has an angle to play.

[[Cadwalader]] is introduced through several transcript variants, including Caldwell, Coldwater, and Caldwalder. His concept is the professional criminal trying to look like a legitimate businessman: street-born, pickpocket-trained, useful with knives, and determined to be taken seriously.

[[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian Luc Benois]] is known in some circles as an engineer and maker of clockwork wonders used in art pieces by the Jewelers Guild. In other circles he is known for forgery, counterfeiting, and the sort of technical work respectable people do not ask about too directly.

[[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet Cossaboon]] is established as a promising Jewelers Guild figure whose family and future are bound up in guild expectations.

By the end of the session, the party has been drawn together at [[The Brass Cog]] for a job connected to Jewelers Guild interests.

## Important Threads

- The Jewelers Guild is looking for deniable help.
- The PCs are all useful but not clean.
- The party's shared ground is not heroism; it is skill, leverage, and opportunity.
- Old Bill / William Burrance is mentioned in the first transcript but explicitly removed at the start of game two.
