# Leandan Chronicle

The Leandan Chronicle is the second Kingdom Divided game. It follows a crew of useful, compromised, and guild-adjacent people pulled into a Jewelers Guild operation about sky crystals, the Airship Guild, and the political balance of the Leandan League.

The game begins in [[Senex]], where feelers are sent into both respectable and less respectable circles. The party is gathered at [[The Brass Cog]] by [[Matthew Rousseau]], who offers them work on behalf of Jewelers Guild interests.

The mission is political as much as practical. A seat on Leandan's Small Council is expected to go to the Shipwrights Guild, but that expected outcome may be disrupted if the Airship Guild can be elevated or separated in the right way. The reason is sky crystals: the unique blue crystals of [[Cataclistos]] make airship travel possible, and whoever controls that flow holds enormous leverage over commerce.

## Player Characters

- [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet Cossaboon]] - Welvart jeweler, gemist, and neophyte sorcerer with deep Jewelers Guild loyalty.
- [[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian Luc Benois]] - artisan, engineer, clockwork maker, and forger of plans and documents.
- [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer Templeton]] - confidence man, social operator, and Templeton Jewelcrafters connection.
- [[Cadwalader]] - professional thief and careful street-trained operator, often heard in transcripts as Caldwell, Coldwater, or Caldwalder.

## Core Mission

The crew is sent to understand what is happening around sky crystals, [[Cataclistos]], the Airship Guild, and the Shipwrights Guild's monopoly over travel and commerce.

They debate whether to enter Cataclistos openly, sneakily, or under cover of business. Their early plan is to present Mercer as an up-and-coming designer or connected guild figure, with Sebastian's inventions and Violet's gem expertise providing enough credibility to get them through doors that would otherwise remain closed.

## Current Arc

The first six sessions carry the party from Senex to sea, through a hurricane, into the Protectorate of Corumia, into contact with Heller giants, and finally toward [[Farisi]], where sea shipping, airships, gems, and Jewelers Guild interests begin to overlap directly.
