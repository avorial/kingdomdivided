# Mikado Game Zero - Character Build

The Mikado Chronicle game zero sessions define the party's themes before play begins.

The players build characters around Mikado houses, Esten exile, Worm God faith, House Maj prophecy, House Tobe reform, and shadow assassination. The table also works through how Mikado culture understands change, genetics, noble house identity, and the relationship between magic and transformation.

## Character Concepts

- [[Mei Ling]]: House Maj noble wrestling with prophecy, speech, and identity.
- [[Nikos]]: changed Worm God missionary with claws and badger-like physicality.
- [[Thorn]]: shadow assassin whose lack of self creates a need for connection.
- [[Diogenes]]: Esten scholar/teacher and cultural interpreter.
- [[Rina]]: noble organizer who gathers the group.
- [[Lorena]]: House Tobe reformist tied to old greatness and new legitimacy.

## Themes

- Change as blessing, danger, and political force.
- Esten culture surviving inside Mikado systems.
- The Belllands' Sedai crisis echoing into Mikado politics.
- Whether old houses can return without repeating old disasters.
