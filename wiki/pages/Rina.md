# Rina

Rina is one of the Mikado Chronicle's noble organizers. She helps draw the party together during the Oldwin opening and operates with enough status to send messages and gather unusual allies.

## Known Threads

- Sends [[Diogenes]] to find [[Thorn]].
- Participates in the opening political meeting before the party is pushed into the larger Mikado arc.
- Becomes part of the group navigating exile, ships, Lumia, and House Hanan politics.

### Aspects

- **Toab Reformationist:** raised on Oren Toab's legends and determined to shame the current shogun, restoring the title to her family from the Abbyeans.
- **Trained Under the Bell:** trained in Oldwyn's Bellringer court, foreign combat styles, etiquette, and libraries, leaving her partially estranged from her homeland.
- **Sword of the Master:** stole the ancient sword of Orin Toab from the Sedi vaults; it inspires Mikado supremacy but could be claimed by a higher-ranking clan member.
- **Fence Mender:** hopes to bridge Bellringers and Mikado kin, risking betrayal in both directions.
- **The Ties that Bind:** family is spread across the country, and Reina is trying to bring them together to repair old wrongs.

### Stunts

- **Friends in High Places:** authorities are likely to arrest Reina when she declares who she is rather than immediately attacking her with violence.
- **Saddle for Two:** no penalty for carrying more than one rider on a horse with her.
- **Power of the Sword Spirit:** when losing a fight while using the sword, spend a Fate Point to negate the effect of one roll; others will hear of the sword's use.
