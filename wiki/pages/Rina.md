# Rina

Rina is one of the Mikado Chronicle's noble organizers. She helps draw the party together during the Oldwin opening and operates with enough status to send messages and gather unusual allies.

## Known Threads

- Sends [[Diogenes]] to find [[Thorn]].
- Participates in the opening political meeting before the party is pushed into the larger Mikado arc.
- Becomes part of the group navigating exile, ships, Lumia, and House Hanan politics.
