# Casimir Durand-Ramirez

## Personal History

Casimir Remirez was born to Anna, a whore in one of the many brothers Connor Silverridge frequented at the Guild of Silk at Icehaven. When he was born she hoped he would be acknowledged, but instead received hush money. Raised by his mother, Casimir never knew his father. She raised him to believe that the only way to survive in the world was through hard work, and that everything needed to be grasped and earned by one's own efforts. Using the payoff money, along with her earnings and influence through clients, was able to acquire him a warrior's training. He was raised with, and eventually fell in love with, his fellow child of a brothel worker, Penelope.

When he was 17, he joined the local garrison. He soon grew frustrated of constantly being challenged over his bastardry. After two years he joined the Crusades as the best chance to gain power and glory. He cared nothing for the religious elements, but learned enough to pretend in front of his zealot superiors. His mind for tactics was noticed and he was recruited as the assistant for Captain Louis de Flours. He quickly became his right hand man, planning and leading raids and assaults. Five years later Louis died, and Casimir leaped at the chance to gain command. Much at the frustration of various nobles, he was able to become captain. He quickly proved himself through the capture of a major settlement. His troops slaughtered the inhabitants in an orgy of violence. He still remembers the dying curse of the Makido wizard as he stabbed him, and every time he loses a hand in cards or rolls poorly with die, he wonders. But worst of all is the screams of children he remembers at night. Liquor and female companionship helps, but does not chase it away. He is well liked by his common troops, who have far more in common with his low birth than most commanders.

Now established, he sends almost all of his money to his mother, who has established herself as a madam of a major brothel. Penelope, know often called Penelope the Peach, works for her, which Casimir is glad for. He wishes he could write better to regularly write them, but unfortunately can not. Casimir dreams of conquering a land and receiving a noble house the way his ancestor Emer Ramirez did. But the Crusades all to often were just raids, with little true land gained. As he gets older, he has started to get frustrated over the lack of true reward over all of his efforts and sacrifice. Despite that, he always does his best to defeat his enemies decisively.
