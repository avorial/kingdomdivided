# Kempenfelt

The Kempenfelt is a ship crewed by Heller giants. It is damaged near [[Davin]] at the same time as [[The Victory]] after the hurricane.

Its captain, [[Ronan]], is badly injured. The local village is reluctant to help the Heller crew, so the party becomes a bridge between the giants and the resources they need.

After the party helps secure medicine, [[Tiberius]] negotiates passage for them toward [[Hodong]] and [[Farisi]].

## Chronicle Use

- Introduces the Heller giants as people rather than rumor.
- Provides passage after the Victory is delayed.
- Gives the party a strong social contact outside normal guild routes.
