# Leandan Session 06 - Farisi and the Airships

## Synopsis

After the medicine is delivered, [[Tiberius]] expresses gratitude and negotiates passage for the party aboard the [[Kempenfelt]]. The ship will carry them toward [[Hodong]], near the end of Saltblood territory on the Cape of [[Farisi]].

The giants explain [[Valens]]' earlier frenzy as a response to the destabilizing fear around their injured leader. [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] draws comparisons to wolf-pack dynamics, helping frame why Ronan's injury affected the crew so deeply.

Before leaving, [[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian]] uses his craft to make a thermometer for a local smith, while [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]] cuts the firestone into several high-quality pieces. Mercer plays one last card game with Captain [[Sarah Van Jansen]], wins a family pearl, and secretly returns it with a note to strengthen the relationship rather than simply take the prize.

On the giant ship, the party enters a world built for people larger than themselves. As they approach Farisi, airships become visible, and the party begins seeking contacts who can explain how airship travel works. Mercer and Violet make contact with [[Adrian]], while Captain Martin and the Dawn's Pearl emerge as a possible route deeper into the airship network.

## Important Threads

- The party now has a relationship with the Heller crew of the Kempenfelt.
- The Victory and Captain Jansen remain valuable contacts.
- Farisi is a key Jewelers Guild and gem region, with major shipping and airship traffic.
- Airship technology depends on expensive gems and trained crews, not just machines.
- The party is getting closer to the sky crystal question that started the mission.
