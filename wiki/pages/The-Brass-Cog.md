# The Brass Cog

The Brass Cog is the inn where [[Matthew Rousseau]] gathers the Leandan party at the start of the chronicle.

The meeting takes place in an upstairs room with drinks, provisions, and enough privacy for people with questionable backgrounds to talk business without doing it in a public cafe.

## Chronicle Use

- First gathering point for the Leandan party.
- Site where the Jewelers Guild job is first formalized.
- Establishes the tone of the group: not clean heroes, but useful people with leverage.
