# Sebastian Luc Benois

Sebastian Luc Benois is a Quintill artisan with a dangerous mixture of delicate craft, counterfeit skill, lockwork knowledge, and romantic conviction.

His past runs through coin clipping, apprenticeship, and serious artisan training. He understands the value of precision, the usefulness of deception, and the safety that comes from appearing deferential while keeping a tool hidden close at hand.

Sebastian presents as a careful craftsperson whose hands and eye are better suited to tools, machines, and fine materials than open violence.

## Beliefs

- His love is his salvation; everything he does is meant to secure a future where they can be together openly.
- Despite past and present wrongs, Sebastian believes in redemption and must keep trying to balance the scales.

## Instincts

- Always keep a spare lockpick hidden in his boot and an obvious tool set at his waist.
- When threatened, use flattery and deference to buy time.
- If a task requires precision, even a trivial one, Sebastian takes time to craft and use a specialized tool.
