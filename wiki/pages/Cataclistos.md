# Cataclistos

Cataclistos is the independent Leandan city-state at the center of the Leandan Chronicle's sky crystal problem.

It was once part of the province of Farisi/Frisii, but gained independence after the discovery of blue sky crystals. Those crystals make airship travel possible and are fiercely guarded by the Airship Guild.

The party's original mission is to find out what is happening around Cataclistos, sky crystals, and the guild politics that flow from that monopoly.
