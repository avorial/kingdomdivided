# Brom Baker

Brom Baker is first mate of [[The Victory]] under [[Sarah Van Jansen]].

He is part of the Shipwrights Guild shipboard network the party first encounters on their way toward [[Farisi]] and [[Cataclistos]].

## Role In The Chronicle

- First mate of the Victory.
- Helps define the practical shipboard world of Leandan travel.
- Present during the hurricane and the damaged landing near Corumia.
