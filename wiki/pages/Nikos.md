# Nikos

Nikos is a changed Mikado character and militant Worm God missionary. He is built around physical transformation, claws, digging, and religious certainty.

His change reads as badger-like: earth-splitting claws, digging instincts, and a body that marks him as blessed or altered by Mikado forces.

## Known Threads

- Uses claws as a primary expression of combat and identity.
- Has a stunt concept around digging and earthwork.
- Becomes a visible example of what it means to be changed in the Mikado sense.
- Interacts with Mikado faith and Esten communities from a place of intense conviction.
