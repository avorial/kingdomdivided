# Maw People

The Maw People are strange beings encountered during the Chabat/Obsidian crisis.

At least one Maw person appears in the underground obsidian area. They do not simply align with the Obsidian. In fact, the party observes that a Maw person may have a greater conflict with the Obsidian than with the party.

## Known Signs

- Connected to the [[Legend of the Maw]] and older Fedor/Maw mythic material.
- One Maw person shatters obsidian and uses pieces as throwing weapons.
- Obsidian and Maw forces seem to oppose each other in at least some contexts.

## Open Questions

- Can the Maw People be negotiated with against the Obsidian?
- Are they native to a Maw world or a buried old passage?
- Why are they appearing now in Mikado lands?
