# Obsidian Taint

The Obsidian Taint is the returning influence of the House of Obsidian and Shadow. It appears in the Mikado Chronicle through altered Esten communities, obsidian surfaces, stolen shadows, missing reflections, and people acting in eerie unity.

The danger reaches back to the Belllands' old Obsidian War, when Obsidian glass and shadow corruption helped reshape rulership and identity.

## Known Signs

- Polished obsidian walls, floors, or coliseum spaces.
- Reflections that show too much or steal too much.
- People with altered eyes or reversed skin.
- Groups acting in unison as if part of one Obsidian world.
- Missing shadows or shadows that have become independent.
- Obsidian shards that can absorb lightning or energy.

## Political Meaning

Some Esten see the return of Obsidian power as a path to independence or restored old rule. Others see it as the same disaster that once consumed the Belllands.

## Open Questions

- Is the Obsidian Taint a disease, a faith, a house magic, or a world pressing through glass?
- Has it compromised the Imperial House?
- Are the [[Maw People]] enemies of the Obsidian, victims of it, or rival predators?
