# Leandan Chronicle Timeline

## Game 1 - The Brass Cog

The party is introduced: [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer Templeton]], [[Cadwalader]], [[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian Luc Benois]], and [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet Cossaboon]] are brought into orbit around a Jewelers Guild job. They gather at [[The Brass Cog]], where [[Matthew Rousseau]] presents the first form of the assignment.

## Game 2 - The Sky Crystal Job

Rousseau explains the political stakes: a Small Council seat, the Shipwrights Guild, the Airship Guild, and the monopoly created by sky crystals from [[Cataclistos]]. The party begins deciding how much they trust one another and how they might enter the city without alarming the people they need to investigate.

## Game 3 - The Victory

The party prepares for travel. They debate cover stories, settle on a boat route around the Cape of [[Farisi]], and board [[The Victory]], a ship captained by [[Sarah Van Jansen]] with [[Brom Baker]] as first mate.

## Game 4 - Stormfall At Corumia

The Victory is caught in a hurricane and blown off course to the Protectorate of Corumia. Damaged but not destroyed, the ship reaches the shallows near [[Davin]], where another ship, the [[Kempenfelt]], is also in trouble.

## Game 5 - The Giant Captain

The party learns the Kempenfelt is crewed by Heller giants and that its half-giant captain, [[Ronan]], is badly injured. [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]] seeks medicine from [[Nadia]] and [[Roya]], while [[Sebastian-Luc-Benois|Sebastian]] helps repair storm damage and [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] pushes for a deal with the giants.

## Game 6 - Farisi And The Airships

After medicine is delivered and [[Tiberius]] negotiates passage, the party boards the Kempenfelt toward [[Hodong]] and [[Farisi]]. On the giant ship and in port, they begin making airship contacts, including [[Adrian]] and Captain Martin of the Dawn's Pearl.
