# Farisi

Farisi is the transcript spelling used for the gem-rich Leandan region described elsewhere as Frisii.

It is important because the Jewelers Guild is strongly settled there, many gems are mined or traded through it, and sea shipping overlaps with airship traffic. For the Leandan party, Farisi becomes the place where the sky crystal mission starts to connect directly to real airship crews and gem commerce.

## Chronicle Use

- Planned route toward [[Cataclistos]].
- Destination after the storm detour through Corumia.
- Region where the party sees multiple airships and starts asking how they operate.
- Home to Jewelers Guild and Smiths interests.
