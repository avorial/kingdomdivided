# Chabat Obsidian Coliseum

The Chabat Obsidian Coliseum is a hidden or buried structure beneath [[Chabat]], reached through seams, descending steps, and obsidian passages.

The place contains polished obsidian surfaces, jail cells, altered figures, and a direct encounter with the [[Obsidian Taint]].

## Known Events

- The party discovers a seam and hidden way down.
- Sandstone steps give way to obsidian.
- A cell contains an Esten woman in Mikado wear.
- Other figures include a Mikado priest, a normal Mikado person, an Obsidian-altered woman, and a Maw person.
- The Maw person shatters obsidian and uses shards as weapons.
- The structure collapses or partly collapses, and there is fear the volcano has become active.

## Significance

The coliseum proves the Obsidian problem is physical, spiritual, and political. It is not only rumor or factional fear; it is present under a living city.
