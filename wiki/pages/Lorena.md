# Lorena

Lorena is a House Tobe reformist character concept from the Mikado Chronicle game zero material.

Her direction is tied to the tension between House Tobe's old success and the need for a new form of Tobe legitimacy. The table shorthand becomes something like "make House Tobe great again," but the deeper point is reform rather than nostalgia alone.

## Known Threads

- Connected to [[House Tobe]].
- Carries or is connected to the ancient sword of Orin Tobe.
- Represents a question about whether old houses can return without repeating old failures.
