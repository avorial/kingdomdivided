# Sarah Van Jansen

Sarah Van Jansen is the captain of [[The Victory]], the Shipwrights Guild vessel that carries the Leandan party away from Senex and toward the route around [[Farisi]].

She is socially sharp and capable at shipboard games. [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] gambles with her during the voyage and later wins a family pearl in a final card game. He returns the pearl secretly with a note, turning the moment into a relationship rather than a simple prize.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Captain of [[The Victory]].
- Provides the party's first major Shipwrights Guild contact.
- Survives the hurricane and damaged landing near Corumia.
- Becomes a possible long-term contact through Mercer's returned pearl.
