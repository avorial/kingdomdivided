# Valens

Valens is a Heller giant from the [[Kempenfelt]].

After [[Ronan]] is badly injured, Valens becomes frenzied while searching for the captain. The party helps de-escalate the moment enough to send him back to the ship.

The giants later explain that Valens' reaction is bound up in the crew's fear and destabilization after their leader is hurt. [[Mercer-Templeton|Mercer]] compares the dynamic to a wolf pack whose leader has been knocked out of place.
