# Mei Ling

Mei Ling is a House Maj character in the [[Mikado Chronicle]]. She is noble, trained in House Maj expectations, and tied to prophecy, spirits, and Worm God religious duty.

Her central personal question is who she becomes when she speaks for no one but herself.

## Known Threads

- Connected to [[House Maj]] and its Worm God role.
- Trained in the language, manners, and expectations of Mikado nobility.
- Recognized as noble by other characters even when the group's status is uncertain.
- Helps navigate Mikado signs, etiquette, and religious gestures.

### Biography

Mei-Ling was born in the jungles of Toab and raised around Imperial Palace intrigue. Her parents served as diplomats for [[House Maj]], and she learned diplomacy by watching the alliances of the Emperor's court. Her poetry earned quiet renown before adulthood, and when an envoy was needed for the distant Bell Lands, Mei-Ling was chosen as a daughter of Mikado and voice for the Emperor.

### Aspects

- **Diplomat of the Salamander Emperor:** always working toward the empowerment and benefit of the Salamander Throne.
- **Bridges Built on Fragile Trust:** cannot stop analyzing people as political actors, even friends, making trust difficult.
- **The Void Calls to Her:** the Worm Void that spoke from the Well of Oldwyn still whispers from shadows and sometimes compels her.
- **Beyond the Empire's Voice:** in new lands and new ideas, Mei-Ling wonders who she is when she speaks only for herself.
- **Bound to the Balance:** marked by House Maj's sacred sigil and burdened with reading omens and preserving harmony between mortal and divine.
