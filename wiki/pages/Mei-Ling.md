# Mei Ling

Mei Ling is a House Maj character in the [[Mikado Chronicle]]. She is noble, trained in House Maj expectations, and tied to prophecy, spirits, and Worm God religious duty.

Her central personal question is who she becomes when she speaks for no one but herself.

## Known Threads

- Connected to [[House Maj]] and its Worm God role.
- Trained in the language, manners, and expectations of Mikado nobility.
- Recognized as noble by other characters even when the group's status is uncertain.
- Helps navigate Mikado signs, etiquette, and religious gestures.
