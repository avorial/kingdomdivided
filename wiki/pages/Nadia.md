# Nadia

Nadia runs an apothecary near [[Davin]] with her daughter [[Roya]].

The party visits the shop after learning that [[Ronan]], captain of the [[Kempenfelt]], is badly injured. Nadia's shop provides a mild green healing potion, a fire starter kit, and an unusually promising uncut fire gemstone.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Supplies medicine for the giant captain.
- Introduces the fire gemstone that [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]] later cuts.
- Shows the small but important local support network after the hurricane.
