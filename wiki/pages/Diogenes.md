# Diogenes

Diogenes is an Esten scholar and teacher in the [[Mikado Chronicle]]. He acts as a bridge between Esten history, Mikado politics, and the party's immediate survival.

He is often the character best positioned to understand Esten rituals, old houses, cultural obligations, and why Lumia's politics are not simply Mikado politics with local decoration.

## Known Threads

- Sent to find [[Thorn]] during the Oldwin opening.
- Has strong Esten cultural knowledge.
- Helps the party navigate [[Lumia]] and [[Chabat]].
- Becomes important when the group investigates the House of Obsidian and Shadow.
