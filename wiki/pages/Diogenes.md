# Diogenes

Diogenes is an Esten scholar and teacher in the [[Mikado Chronicle]]. He acts as a bridge between Esten history, Mikado politics, and the party's immediate survival.

He is often the character best positioned to understand Esten rituals, old houses, cultural obligations, and why Lumia's politics are not simply Mikado politics with local decoration.

## Known Threads

- Sent to find [[Thorn]] during the Oldwin opening.
- Has strong Esten cultural knowledge.
- Helps the party navigate [[Lumia]] and [[Chabat]].
- Becomes important when the group investigates the House of Obsidian and Shadow.

### Aspects

- **Tested to the Bell:** a former Tested child who left that outlawed life, rose through imperial bureaucracy, and now speaks with the voice of the King of Bells; surviving Tested may see him as a traitor.
- **Too Smart for Friends:** his intellect cuts through politics and strategy, but confidence reads as arrogance and leaves him isolated.
- **Friends for Today:** teaches Rina to understand Belland customs and bridge cultures while quietly imagining a future led by Esten wisdom.
- **Angalaglassi (Obsidian):** claimed through Esten Naming by the forbidden House of Obsidian, with glass spirits whispering for counsel and offerings.
- **Second Chances:** haunted by Mercy's failure during the Bellands' fall to the Sedi; aided by old gods in his desire to push down the Salamander Throne.
