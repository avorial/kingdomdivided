# Leandan Dig Site

The Leandan Dig Site is a major concern in the Lumia portion of the Mikado Chronicle.

The party learns that Leandan workers, guildists, or diggers are involved in a large excavation in or near Esten lands. The work appears to have papers connected to [[House Hanan]], but its legality and purpose are suspect.

## Known Clues

- Guildists are not normally allowed free travel in the Mikado Empire.
- The dig is described as one of the largest or most ambitious digs in Mikado lands.
- House Hanan papers or seals appear connected to travel authority.
- The dig may connect to old Maw spaces, Obsidian sites, or buried Esten history.
- Later orders suggest all digging should be stopped until records are properly reviewed.

## Open Questions

- Who authorized the dig?
- What were they trying to uncover?
- Was House Hanan truly involved, forged, or manipulated?
- Did the dig awaken the [[Obsidian Taint]] or merely expose it?
