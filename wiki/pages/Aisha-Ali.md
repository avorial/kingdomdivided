# Aisha Ali

Aisha Ali is a trader out of Rahabriz with a trading route to Kithnos.

After the hurricane strands the party near [[Davin]], she is mentioned as a possible transportation option that could help the group return toward [[Farisi]] and the original Cataclistos route.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Possible transportation contact.
- Connects the storm detour back to the larger Leandan trade network.
