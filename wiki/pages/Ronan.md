# Ronan

Ronan is the half-giant captain of the [[Kempenfelt]].

He is badly injured during the hurricane near Corumia. [[Violet-Cossaboon|Violet]] suspects a concussion and seeks medicine even though she is not a doctor. The party's attempt to help Ronan becomes the basis for a relationship with the Heller crew.

## Role In The Chronicle

- Injured captain whose condition destabilizes the Kempenfelt crew.
- The reason [[Tiberius]] and the other giants are willing to negotiate.
- His injury helps reveal local prejudice against Heller giants.
