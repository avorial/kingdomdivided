# Spedgenium

Spedgenium is a small coastal town encountered early in the Mikado Chronicle.

The party begins one session here after selling goods, buying or arranging a cart, and preparing to travel inland through Esten-controlled or Esten-majority territory.

## Known Events

- The party starts in town after arriving on the coast.
- They sell goods to secure transportation.
- Esten guards and House Hanan interests begin to shape their movement.
